Installing BioCraft
===================

Download the installer for your computer from https://github.com/vinc395/BioCraft/releases:
BioCraft_<version>_x64-setup.exe for Windows, or BioCraft_<version>_universal.dmg for a Mac. Each
is about 600 MB, because the icon library is inside. Neither is signed by a registered developer,
so the first time, Windows and macOS ask you to confirm that you want to open it (below).

Windows 10 or 11
  1. Run BioCraft_<version>_x64-setup.exe. It installs for you only: no administrator rights needed.
  2. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC", because the installer isn't signed by a
     registered publisher. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
  3. Open BioCraft from the Start menu. .biocraft figures now open in BioCraft when double-clicked.
  To remove it: Settings › Apps › Installed apps › BioCraft › Uninstall. Your figures (Documents\
  BioCraft figures) and settings stay.

macOS 12 Monterey or later (Apple silicon or Intel)
  1. Open BioCraft_<version>_universal.dmg and drag BioCraft into Applications.
  2. The first time, macOS may refuse to open it, because it isn't notarised by Apple:
     - macOS 15 Sequoia or later: try to open it once, then go to System Settings › Privacy &
       Security, scroll down and click "Open Anyway".
     - Earlier versions: right-click (or Control-click) BioCraft in Applications, choose Open, then
       Open again.
     If macOS says the app "is damaged", run this once in Terminal:
       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/BioCraft.app
  To remove it: drag BioCraft from Applications to the Bin. Your figures (Documents/BioCraft
  figures) and settings stay.

Your figures are ordinary .biocraft files in Documents › BioCraft figures; Home shows them, and they
save themselves as you work. The icons come from open collections: THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt (in the
app's folder) lists their licences, and the Credits tab tells you what a figure needs to credit.
